Shoalwater is a boat-and-drone game drawn over the real seafloor of Sitka Sound. Press launch and the boat casts off on her own — she'll work the shoal, hunt a lost crab pot, and feel her way into the cove. The moment you touch a key, a stick, anything — she's yours, exactly where she floats.

01It plays itself

The autopilot drives the same controls you do — same throttle, same physics, no cheats and no rails. It's a real player made of code: it reads the keel depth, works the drone on a battery of thirds, and creeps into shallow water the way a careful skipper would.

02Touch anything — she's yours

Any key, tap or drag takes over instantly, mid-motion, wherever she lies. No pause, no handover screen. When you want to watch again, the ▶ Watch the demo button hands her back. Learning to play starts with watching her play.

03She has a job

Scene 1: cast off, make Jamestown Bay, work the shoal with the drone — and find the Marigold's lost crab pot, homing on its pinger. Log the wreck in the cove for the ★, then bring her home. Mind the keel: two hard groundings and she's taking water.

Where this is going: Coach Player

This demo is the seed of something bigger. Coach Player turns the autopilot into an avatar with a personality — one that studies how the best players play, develops its own style, narrates what it's doing, and takes direction from you, the coach: you set the plan, nudge its dials, and grab the wheel in the tight spots.

The design is public and in progress — two independent AI design passes, a question database of what makes play worth watching, and a runtime that lets the avatar write its own new moves under supervision. Follow it on GitHub →
