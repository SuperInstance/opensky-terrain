# Scene 1: Jamestown Bay

*Open Sitka Sound — first playable scene. Every depth in this script is a
measured depth from the Sitka Sound pack (Jamestown Bay centre 9.3 m,
mid-bay shoal patch 3.7 m, back-of-bay shore 1.7 m, boatyard shore
0.7 m; tide range −1.5 m to +4.0 m over the MLLW bottom). Drafted with
fleet assistance; facts verified line by line before publication.*

### Scene Premise

Jamestown Bay is a quiet, wooded bay on the edge of Sitka Sound —
islets, private docks, float houses, a boatyard on its west shore.
The captain brings a small dive boat in over the mid-bay shoal to do
the oldest job in the world: go and look. The chart suggests what is
down there. The scene is about the difference between what the chart
suggests and what the captain has actually seen.

---

### Opening Beat

The dive boat rides at anchor over the shoal patch mid-bay — 3.7 m of
water under the keel, the deeper bay (9.3 m at its centre) all around.
Wooded islets stand off in every direction: Cannon, Guertin, Dove.

**On-screen text:**
```
ANCHORED — JAMESTOWN BAY SHOAL
DEPTH UNDER KEEL: 3.7 M
DRONE BATTERY: FULL
```

**Captain's Log:**
```
The chart says there is a shoal here, and the sounder agrees. What the
chart cannot say is what lives on it. That part you have to go and see.
```

---

### Objectives

1. **Work the shoal.** Take the drone down to the 3.7 m patch and look
   at what is actually there.
2. **Find the wreck.** A small wreck lies in the cove at the back of
   the bay. Reach it, circle it, come home — on the rule of thirds:
   a third of the battery out, a third back, a third never spent.
3. **Mind the gate.** The back of the bay is 1.7 m water over chart
   datum. What floated in on the tide waits for the tide to leave.

---

### The Dive

The drone goes over the side and sinks the short distance to the
shoal. There is not much water here — less than 4 m, surface to
bottom — and the light comes all the way down with it.

**Drone HUD:**
```
DEPTH: 3.1 M · CLEARANCE: 0.6 M
BATTERY: FULL
```

Kelp stands on the patch, swaying in what little current there is.
Crabs work the bottom between the fronds. None of this is on the
chart. The chart only knew the shape; the looking adds the life.

**Captain's Log:**
```
Shoal surveyed: kelp, crabs, good light to the bottom. The confidence
here is no longer the chart's. It is ours.
```

Out toward the back of the bay, past the point, the bottom rises
toward the little cove — and something in the shallows is the wrong
shape for a rock.

---

### The Wreck Beat

The transit is a shallowing road: 9.3 m water gives way to 4, to 3,
to the cove's quiet pocket where the float houses stand. The drone's
clearance shrinks as the bottom rises to meet it. And there, in the
shallows at the back of the bay, is the wreck — a small vessel,
settled, encrusted, entirely at home.

**Drone HUD:**
```
DEPTH: 2.2 M · CLEARANCE: 0.8 M
BATTERY: 2/3
```

One slow circuit, headlight on, is what the battery budget bought.
The choice the scene teaches: a second circuit spends the reserve.
Discipline says the reserve is not yours to spend on curiosity.

**Captain's Log:**
```
Wreck found and circled, once, properly. She will keep. So will we —
that is the point of the third you do not spend.
```

---

### The Return

The drone comes home up the shallowing road in reverse and is
recovered with its third unspent. Then the bay sets its second
lesson: the boat nosed into the back of the bay on a rising tide,
and the back of the bay is 1.7 m over datum. Until the water returns,
the mud owns the shoreline and the channel is a rumour.

**On-screen text:**
```
TIDE: RISING
THE GATE OPENS WHEN THE WATER SAYS SO
```

**Captain's Log:**
```
We wait the way everyone here waits — kettle on, watching the water
come back over the flats. The tide is not an obstacle in this game.
The tide is the clock the whole coast lives by.
```

---

### Debrief

What the captain knows at the end of Scene 1 is different in kind
from what the chart knew at the start of it. The shoal has kelp and
crabs on it now — seen, not inferred. The wreck has a position, a
shape, and exactly one honest circuit of film. The bay's exit has a
condition attached: the tide. Knowledge in this world is not
collected. It is witnessed, timestamped, and carried home inside a
battery budget — which is to say, it is earned exactly the way it is
earned on the real water.
