The finding that started it

Sitka's tide station is 9451600.

A widely copied research pack called NOAA station 9452210 “Sitka” in its spec, its generator, its ground truth, and all four of its audit implementations. That station is Juneau. Sitka is 9451600; the number in the pack's bake memo, 9451054, is Port Alexander. Verified against the NOAA/NCEI CO-OPS station tables.

And the plateau that wasn't the algorithms' fault

Four independent tide-correction implementations plateaued at ≈ 0.40 fathoms of disagreement on a test shoal. The tide table ended at 23:54 while the sounding tracks ran to 03:37 — and the interpolator silently clamped ~300 out-of-range soundings. The fix is an invariant, now in the spec: tide tables must cover the full timestamp range of every sounding file; never clamp — exclude and flag.

What you're looking at

  • Colour bands are the contour ladder. Each band is one contour interval of the selected mode — the same intervals the LOD selector contract binds to mesh accuracy, so a finer mode never pretends to resolution the tiles don't have.
  • Confidence tint (toggle) washes out vertices whose soundings were interpolated rather than measured — the extensions baked into every tile (id 5 bottom-type, id 6 confidence) rendered, not just stored.
  • The water is the runtime's job. The bake is static MLLW seafloor; the tide slider moves the sea, exactly as the spec splits it. Relief ×2 is render-only — it can never leak into the data.
  • Hover the bottom for a spot readout: depth in fathoms, confidence, bottom type at the nearest vertex.
